About This Policy

Samsung TV Remote is a general-purpose utility that discovers and controls compatible Samsung TVs over the user's local network. This policy explains what information the app accesses, what remains on the device, what is sent to our service providers, and the choices available to you.

The app does not require a user-created account to control a TV. An anonymous Firebase identifier is created only when the in-app support chat is used so that the conversation can be associated with the same installation.

Information We Collect

Information you provide directly

  • TV and device setup information: a TV name, IP address, MAC address when supplied or discovered, protocol and port, discovery aliases, and pairing or session credentials needed to reconnect to a TV. These values are stored locally on your Android device.
  • Support messages: if you use Support Chat, the message text and message timestamps are sent to our Firebase Cloud Firestore support system. Do not include passwords, payment information, PINs, or other information you do not want support personnel to receive.
  • Preferences: settings such as language, theme, notification choices, navigation mode, saved-device choices, and other app preferences are stored locally.

Information collected automatically by the app and its services

  • Usage and app events: screen/session activity and technical events such as discovery, connection, pairing, command, update, and error outcomes. Event parameters are designed to use low-cardinality technical labels; the app does not intentionally send TV IP addresses, MAC addresses, pairing PINs, auth tokens, or raw WebSocket data to Analytics.
  • Device and app information: app version, Android version, device manufacturer and model, language/locale, time zone or country information, and technical diagnostics used for analytics, update delivery, and crash investigation.
  • Identifiers: Google Analytics app-instance identifiers, Firebase Installation ID, Crashlytics installation UUID, and an anonymous Firebase Authentication user ID for Support Chat. Firebase Analytics may also access the Android Advertising ID unless that collection is disabled by the app configuration or by Android.
  • Coarse location signals: Google Analytics may derive general or approximate location from a masked IP address. The app itself does not request precise-location or approximate-location runtime permissions and does not use location to provide TV control.
  • Crash information: crash stack traces, relevant app state, device metadata, developer-defined crash keys, and breadcrumbs immediately before a crash. Crash keys are intended to exclude IP addresses, MAC addresses, PINs, and authentication tokens.

Information collected for support diagnostics

When Support Chat sends a message, or when support requests a fresh diagnostic snapshot, the support record may include the Android manufacturer and model, Android version, app version, number and brand/platform summary of saved TVs, network transport and a network-problem category, locale, approximate time since installation, and the Firebase Analytics app-instance ID. The diagnostic snapshot is designed not to include TV IP addresses, MAC addresses, TV names, raw message content, pairing credentials, or authentication tokens.

Permissions and local-network access

The app uses Internet and network-state access to communicate with Firebase and compatible TVs; Wi-Fi and multicast access to discover TVs; a connected-device foreground service and wake lock to keep an active session working; notification access for the remote-control notification; and boot-completed access to restore the app's quick-control state. The Android build also includes the Advertising ID and related Google services permissions through Firebase Analytics. No contacts, camera, microphone, phone number, SMS, or precise-location permission is requested by the audited release manifest.

Google Play Data Safety: the Play Console declaration must cover the complete behavior of every currently distributed version, including data sent by SDKs. For this build, review at least app activity, app information and performance, device or other identifiers, approximate/general location derived by Analytics, support-message content, and anonymous authentication data. Local-only TV records do not constitute off-device collection until they are transmitted.

How We Use Information

  • To discover, pair with, reconnect to, and control compatible TVs on your local network.
  • To save your selected TV and preferences so the app works across sessions.
  • To provide Support Chat, investigate connection problems, and respond to support requests.
  • To measure app usage, diagnose crashes, improve reliability, and understand which connection paths need improvement.
  • To deliver remote configuration and version-update notices, maintain service security, prevent abuse, and comply with law.

For users in the EEA, the UK, and Switzerland, the legal basis depends on the purpose: performance of the requested app service or support service; our legitimate interests in security, reliability, and product improvement; compliance with legal obligations; and consent where consent is required, including for any future personalized advertising or other optional processing.

Third-Party Services

The current Android project and release build use the following Google Firebase services. They process data on our behalf or provide the technical service described below:

  • Google Analytics for Firebase — app-instance ID, Advertising ID where available, app lifecycle and usage events, device/app information, and coarse location derived from masked IP. Used for product analytics and reliability analysis. See Google Analytics data collection.
  • Firebase Crashlytics — crash reports, relevant app/device metadata, installation identifiers, developer-defined keys, and Analytics breadcrumbs. Used to diagnose crashes and stability issues. See Firebase Crashlytics.
  • Firebase Remote Config — app/package and version information, platform and OS information, language, country, time zone, Firebase app ID, and installation-related identifiers. Used to deliver update and feature configuration. See Firebase Remote Config.
  • Firebase Authentication — an anonymous user ID, Firebase app ID, IP address, and technical user-agent information used to create and protect the anonymous Support Chat session. No email, password, or social-login account is used by the audited app. See Firebase Authentication.
  • Cloud Firestore — Support Chat messages, timestamps, the support diagnostics described above, and the anonymous Firebase user ID used to scope the support thread. See Cloud Firestore.

For more information about Firebase's privacy and security practices, see Privacy and Security in Firebase and Google's Privacy Policy.

Advertising and Advertising ID

The audited source, Gradle dependency graph, merged release manifest, and release APK do not contain the Google Mobile Ads SDK, Google UMP SDK, or an AdMob mediation adapter. Accordingly, this audited version does not currently serve ads through AdMob.

Firebase Analytics does bring the Android Advertising ID permission into the merged release manifest and may collect the Advertising ID by default for Analytics. You can reset or delete the Advertising ID, or restrict its use, in Android settings. You can also contact us about disabling applicable Analytics collection.

The project brief describes Google AdMob mediation as intended. If a Play-distributed build actually includes AdMob or mediation partners, this policy and the Play Data Safety declaration must be updated before that build is published to name Google Mobile Ads, each active mediation partner, the data each partner processes, and the applicable consent choices. For AdMob requirements, see Google Mobile Ads data disclosure and Google's EU/UK/Swiss consent requirements.

Data Sharing and Disclosure

We share information only as described in this policy:

  • With Google and Firebase service providers to provide Analytics, Crashlytics, Remote Config, Authentication, and Firestore.
  • With authorized Bluespacestudioz support personnel when you use Support Chat, so they can read and answer your message and its attached diagnostic summary.
  • When necessary to comply with law, protect the rights or safety of users or the service, investigate abuse, or respond to a lawful request.
  • In connection with a merger, acquisition, financing, or sale of all or part of the business, subject to applicable confidentiality and legal requirements.

Bluespacestudioz does not sell your support messages, saved-TV records, or support diagnostics, and does not share them with third parties for their own direct marketing. Google and Firebase may process identifiers and technical data under their own terms and privacy policies to provide their services, maintain security, measure usage, and comply with law.

Data Retention and Deletion

  • Local TV records and preferences: retained on your device until you remove the saved TV, clear the app's data, or uninstall the app. Pairing/session credentials are removed when the associated saved TV is removed, subject to normal device backup or operating-system behavior.
  • Support Chat: active support threads are retained while needed to provide support. Closed support threads and messages are automatically deleted under the Firebase Cloud Firestore time-to-live configuration after the configured support-retention period. A deletion request may be made at any time.
  • Crashlytics: Firebase states that crash stack traces and associated identifiers are retained for 90 days before removal begins, subject to Google's current service operation and backup processes.
  • Analytics and Remote Config: retained according to the Firebase project settings and Google's service practices for as long as needed for analytics, security, troubleshooting, and service operation. Aggregated or de-identified reports may remain after source records are deleted.

Retention may be longer where required for legal claims, security investigations, fraud prevention, or legal compliance.

Children's Privacy

Samsung TV Remote is a general-purpose utility and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information through Support Chat, contact us so we can investigate and delete it when appropriate.

The Play Console content rating, target-audience declaration, and actual audience must accurately describe the app. If the app is targeted to children or to a mixed audience that includes children, Google Play Families requirements may apply to the app, its support feature, Analytics, and any future advertising SDKs.

Data Security

Firebase traffic is sent using encrypted HTTPS/TLS connections, and Firebase states that its relevant services encrypt data in transit and, where applicable, at rest. Local records are stored inside Android's app sandbox. The app's Room database stores pairing/session credentials locally and does not intentionally log them or attach them to support diagnostics.

Communication with a TV occurs over the local network using the protocol supported by that TV. Some local protocols may not use transport encryption, so use the app only on a network you trust. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Information sent to Firebase or Google may be processed in India, the United States, or other countries where Google or its service providers operate. Those countries may have data-protection rules different from your country. We rely on the service providers' applicable data-processing terms, contractual safeguards, and other legally recognized transfer mechanisms where required.
